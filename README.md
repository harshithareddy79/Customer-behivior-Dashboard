# Retail Customer Behavior Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project is an end-to-end data analytics project that analyzes retail customer shopping behavior. The goal is to identify purchasing patterns and provide business insights that help improve customer engagement, marketing strategies, and business decisions.

## Business Problem
The company wanted to understand how customer demographics, subscription status, payment methods, shipping preferences, and product categories influence purchasing behavior.

## Tools Used
- Python (Pandas, NumPy)
- PostgreSQL
- SQL
- Power BI
- Jupyter Notebook

## Data Cleaning (Python)
- Loaded and explored the dataset.
- Filled missing values in Review Rating using the median within each product category.
- Renamed columns using snake_case.
- Created a new Age Group feature.
- Converted purchase frequency into numeric values.
- Removed redundant columns.

## SQL Analysis
Business questions answered include:
- Total revenue by gender
- Revenue by age group
- Subscriber vs non-subscriber analysis
- Top-rated products
- Revenue by shipping type
- Revenue by payment method
- Customer segmentation
- Discount analysis

## Power BI Dashboard
The dashboard includes:
- KPI Cards
  - Average Purchase Amount
  - Total Revenue
  - Total Orders
  - Subscription Rate
  - Average Review Rating
  - Revenue Per Customer

- Interactive Charts
  - Revenue by Category
  - Revenue by Payment Method
  - Revenue by Age Group
  - Revenue by Shipping Type
  - Subscription Status Distribution
  - Seasonal Revenue and Orders

- Interactive Filters
  - Subscription Status
  - Gender
  - Category
  - Shipping Type

## Key Business Insights
- Clothing generated the highest revenue.
- Young Adults contributed the highest revenue.
- Most customers are non-subscribers.
- Credit Card and PayPal were the most preferred payment methods.
- Free Shipping generated the highest revenue among shipping methods.

## Business Recommendations
- Increase subscription adoption through loyalty programs.
- Focus marketing campaigns on high-performing product categories.
- Improve fast shipping options.
- Promote popular payment methods to improve customer experience.
